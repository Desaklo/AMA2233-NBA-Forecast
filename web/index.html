How the estimates are made

No leakage

Every feature is known before tip-off: rest and back-to-backs, pre-game season win percentage and point differential, last-5 and last-10 form, offensive and defensive ratings, and home/road splits. Same-game box scores are never used as inputs.

Temporal split

Models are trained on the 2021–22 through 2024–25 regular seasons (4,920 games). 2025–26 is held out for testing. 2026–27 games are a forward application and were not used to fit or tune the models.

Two models

Logistic regression (L2-regularized, the default) is the interpretable linear model from the course syllabus. A random forest is shown as a comparison. Both are scored with accuracy, log-loss, and the Brier score against home-court and win-percentage baselines.

What is missing

Injuries, travel, roster changes, and referees are not in the model. Early-season estimates are noisier because there is little in-season history. These are educational estimates, not betting advice.
